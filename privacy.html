Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This policy explains how Vistro ("we", "us") handles information on this website, vistro.dev. Our apps have their own privacy policies, linked from each app's page on the App Store.

What this website collects

This website does not ask you to create an account, sign in, fill in forms or leave comments. It does not use advertising, tracking pixels or analytics scripts, and it does not set cookies.

Server logs

The site is hosted by Vercel Inc. When you load a page, Vercel's servers automatically receive technical data such as your IP address, browser and device type, the page requested and the time. This is used to deliver the site, keep it secure and prevent abuse. We do not use it to identify you, and we do not combine it with other data. See Vercel's privacy policy for how it handles this data.

Links to other sites

This site links to the Apple App Store and to numfall.store. Those services are run by other parties and have their own privacy practices. We are not responsible for them.

Our apps

What an app collects, if anything, is described in that app's own privacy policy and in the App Privacy section of its App Store page. This policy covers only the vistro.dev website.

Emailing us

If you email support@vistro.dev, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply to you, and keep it only as long as needed to deal with your request.

Your rights

You can ask us what personal data we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing support@vistro.dev. We will respond within a reasonable time and as required by applicable law, including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Children

This website is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy: support@vistro.dev.
