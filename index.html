NumFall

Drop the numbers. Beat the clock.

A timed number puzzle where numbers fall under gravity. Place each one so every row, column and box holds each number once, before the clock runs out.

  • Always against the clock. The timer is on for every level.
  • Three boards. Quick 4×4, Classic 6×6 and Master 9×9.
  • No ads. Free to download and play.
  • Made for iPhone and iPad. Sharp at every size, smooth at 120 Hz.
